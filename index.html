University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)

Hi, I’m Shuting Pang. I am a Master’s student in Computer Science and Technology at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), supervised by Prof. Xiaoshuang Shi. I received my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from Sichuan University (SCU).

My research focuses on medical foundation models, multimodal learning, and interpretable AI. I am particularly interested in building medical AI systems that can learn from heterogeneous healthcare data, generalize across real-world clinical settings, and provide clinically meaningful interpretations.

My recent work studies cross-age and cross-setting transfer for adolescent mental-health assessment, feature-interpretable learning from structured clinical data, and interpretable representation learning for medical imaging.

🤖 Research

Cross-Age and Cross-Setting Visual Foundation Models for Mental Health

Sep. 2025 – Present · UESTC × West China Hospital, Sichuan University
  • Domain-aware semi-supervised learning for transferring knowledge from adult clinical and unlabeled adolescent community data to data-scarce adolescent clinical settings.
  • Visual foundation models for robust mental-health assessment across age groups and clinical-to-community settings.
  • Interpretable analysis across interview questions, temporal segments, and anatomically defined facial regions.
  • Improved adolescent depression AUC from 80.03% to 86.64%, with further evaluation on suicidal behavior and few-shot community screening.

LLMs for Structured Clinical Data: Adaptation and Feature Interpretation

Sep. 2024 – Jun. 2026 · UESTC × West China Hospital, Sichuan University
  • Learnable feature-wise embeddings that adapt text-pretrained LLMs to structured clinical data while preserving numerical semantics.
  • Dynamic inter-feature dependency modeling for joint prediction and feature selection.
  • Achieved 77.63% AUC with feature importance consistent with established clinical knowledge.

Interpretable Representation Learning for Medical Imaging Diagnosis

May 2023 – May 2025 · UESTC × West China-SenseTime Joint Laboratory, SCU
  • Hierarchical attention learning across slice, region, and channel levels for interpretable 3D MRI classification.
  • Clinically inspired consistency learning aligning 2D and 3D attention.
  • Improved diagnostic accuracy by 4.4% on real-world clinical data while enhancing interpretation consistency.
